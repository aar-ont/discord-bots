# Hiring-server intro messages

Read each server's rules first. Most have a specific **#for-hire** / **#portfolio** channel, a required format, and a posting cooldown. Follow it exactly or your post gets removed.

## Standard "for hire" post
```
**[FOR HIRE] Discord Bot Developer – discord.js**

Hi! I build custom Discord bots: tickets, moderation, economy/leveling, giveaways, polls, reaction roles, and custom commands.

🎫 Ticket/Mod bot demo: <link>
💰 Economy/Leveling demo: <link>
🛠️ Utility bot demo: <link>
📂 GitHub: <link>

💵 Simple bots from $15 · Custom features $50–150 · Hosting $5–10/mo
⚡ Usually delivered in 1–3 days, with a video preview before final payment
🔒 I never need your account password. You own your bot and token.

DM me with what you need!
```

## Short version (for servers with character limits)
```
[FOR HIRE] discord.js bot dev – tickets, mod, economy, giveaways, custom commands. From $15, hosting available. Demos + GitHub: <link>. DMs open!
```

## Replying to a [HIRING] post
```
Hey! Saw your post about <their need>. I've built something very close to this. Here's a demo: <most relevant link>.
I could have <specific feature they asked for> done in about <N> days for $<X>. Happy to send a quick preview clip before you pay the rest. Want me to go ahead?
```
Reply fast and point to the **one** demo that matches what they asked for. That wins more jobs than a generic pitch.
