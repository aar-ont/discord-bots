# Pricing & payments

## Price list
| Tier | Price | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Simple bot** | $15–40 | Welcome + autorole, a few custom commands, one of my demo bots set up for your server |
| **Custom features** | $50–150 | Ticket system with custom categories, economy with a custom shop, integrations (Twitch/YouTube alerts, Google Sheets), anything that needs design work |
| **Hosting & upkeep** | $5–10 / month per bot | I keep it online 24/7, restart it if it crashes, and do small fixes |

Starting out, price toward the low end until you have 3–5 good reviews, then raise prices.

## Getting paid: Cash App
- **Cash App needs 18+ for a regular account.** Ages 13–17 need a **sponsored account** that a parent approves and oversees. Or use a parent's Cash App. Check Cash App's current rules in the app before your first job.
- **Cash App only works in the US and UK.** Lots of Discord buyers are overseas. For them, either pass or take the job through BuiltByBit/Fiverr, which handle payment for you.
- **Get paid before you deliver.** Cash App payments can't be reversed, which protects you. It also means buyers take a risk, so offer:
  - **Under $40:** 100% upfront.
  - **$40 and up:** 50% upfront, 50% before you hand over the final code or turn on hosting.
  - Send a **short demo video of their bot working in your test server** before the second payment. That builds trust.
- Include your `$Cashtag` and the **exact amount** in the quote. Ask them to put the order name in the note (e.g. "TicketBot – CoolServer").
- **Scam warnings:**
  - Never send money *to* a buyer.
  - Ignore "I overpaid, refund the difference."
  - Ignore "I'll pay after it's done" from new accounts.
  - Never click "payment verification" links. Real Cash App payments show up in the app, not in DMs.
- Keep a simple spreadsheet: date, client, what you built, amount, paid Y/N. You'll want it at tax time.
